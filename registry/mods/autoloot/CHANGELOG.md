## 1.1.0
- The Autoloot window has a button in the option menu: press Esc and choose **Autoloot**, next to the other settings. Alt+O still opens it too.
- The menu button needs app 1.5.0 or later; on older apps only Alt+O opens the window.

## 1.0.0
- An Autoloot window (Alt+O): loot by rarity, by item type and a list of up to 100 exact items, with a monster check that shows what your settings would pick up.
- Five presets on the character, kept across logins and computers.
- Works where @autoloot is kept for GMs, and the item list survives logging out.
