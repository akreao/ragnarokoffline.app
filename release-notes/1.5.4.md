Mods that say which game client they're for, an optional companion strategy system for mods, and gamepad support for your party, plus fixes for forged item names, the Attendance Check window, copied skills in the skill tree, guild emblems and companion healing.

## New
- **Which client you have:** Settings → General now shows which game client your files are from, such as kRO or iRO (read from your data.grf), and so do diagnostics. (#477)
- **Gamepad: party support, target categories and emotes:** D-pad up and down now switch what the right stick targets (monsters, NPCs, items, or your party). In the new Support category the right stick opens a radial of you and your party, showing each member's HP and your SP, to heal and buff them, with out-of-range members marked and the dead only offered revives. Holding Menu opens an emote grid with favourites. L3 now sits or stands, and holding the right stick recenters the cursor. Thanks @BlaXun. (roBrowserLegacy#93 via #481)

## Modding
- **Mods for one client:** a mod can say which client's files it was made for, with `"requires": { "client": "kRO" }` in its mod.json. On another client it isn't applied, and Settings → Mods says why. A mod that works everywhere but needs different files per client can use `clientFolders`, like the renewal and pre-renewal folders. See [Mods for one client](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/blob/main/docs/MODDING.md#mods-for-one-client). Thanks @akreao for tracking down the kRO-only mod that broke an iRO cash shop. (#477)
- **Your own copy of a GitHub mod:** a mod the list publishes from its author's repository, but installed some other way (an author's working copy, or a zip), is now labelled "Your own copy" in Settings → Mods. It isn't checked for updates, and the label says so and how to get its releases from Find Mods instead. (#479)
- **Companion strategies (experimental):** a mod can now teach companions how to fight, per monster, per job and per build, with phases they switch between and signals between companions, in a new `db/population_strategy.yml`. Two example mods show it: companion-roles (what each kind of companion does at any boss and against undead, demons and ghosts) and companion-tactics (Phreeoni's phases, Monk and Ninja builds against the Stalactic Golem). With no mod providing plans nothing changes. See [Companion strategies](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/tree/main/docs/mods/companion-strategies). Thanks @BlaXun. (#480)

## Fixes
- **Companions and Sanctuary:** companions no longer place Sanctuary where it would heal the monsters hitting them, and healers pass over an ally their heals can't help (an undead-armoured one) instead of wasting every cast on it. Thanks @BlaXun. (#480)
- **Guild emblems:** the asset server now passes a guild emblem on only once all of it has arrived. Thanks @akreao. (RemoteClient-Rust#7 via #478)
- **Forged item names:** forged and brewed items showed their crafter's name as raw markup in vending shops, and in trade, equipment and other item windows. The name now shows in colour, and as plain text in chat. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#94 via #487)
- **Attendance Check window:** for a server or mod that runs attendance, the window now opens once the map has loaded instead of behind the loading screen, shows its reward icons, and marks the day you claimed on its own slot. It also no longer says there is no attendance event on every map change. Thanks @MondoTruth. (roBrowserLegacy#95 via #487)
- **Copied skills in the skill tree:** a skill copied with Plagiarism or Reproduce no longer takes skill points that the server then hands back, and a skill copied in game counts as learned straight away, including after Reset. Thanks @MondoTruth. (roBrowserLegacy#97 via #489)
