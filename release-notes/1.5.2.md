A map editor, headless hosting, companion rebirth, navigation from your server's own NPCs, monsters and warps, mods that can direct the population's players and keep data of their own, and the full English translation for newer windows, plus fixes for mercenaries drawn as only a head, switching between renewal and pre-renewal, fast double clicks in shops, the Game Start button and the navigation preview.

## New
- **Map editor:** Settings → Tools → Map editor makes a new map, or a copy of one of your client's, and saves it as a mod: ground and textures, walkability, water and light, models, NPCs, warps, monsters, sky, weather and music. **Test in game** switches the mod on and puts your character there. AI agents can drive it too, through a command line and MCP tools on the same local address as the game agent, and Settings → Play with an AI agent can turn that off. See the [Map editor guide](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/blob/main/docs/mods/MAP_EDITOR.md). (#422, #442, #444, #445)
- **Headless mode (experimental):** start the app with `--headless` to host a world with no windows at all, on macOS, Windows or Linux, with Settings opening in a browser. It's for a spare machine, a home server or a cloud box. See [Running headless](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/blob/main/docs/HEADLESS.md). (#419)
- **More of the game in English:** with the English translation on, the Reputation window, character select, the Laphine and Enchant windows and other newer windows now show English art and names instead of kRO's Korean. The translation now adds ROenglishRE's newer layers up to the client version you picked in Settings. Thanks @akreao. (#450)
- **Navigation in English:** the navigation search now lists maps, NPCs and monsters by their English names with a kRO client, using every map your own client has. Before, it was in Korean unless you turned on the older English tables, which miss about a quarter of today's maps. Thanks @akreao. (#449)
- **Navigation from the server itself (optional):** three new bundled mods, off by default, make the navigation window match the server you're playing on instead of kRO. Navigation Server NPCs lists the NPCs the server and your mods actually place, Navigation Server Monsters lists their spawns with the server's own levels and stats, and Navigation Server Warps routes between maps over the server's own portals, so pre-renewal towns and mods' maps route correctly. Thanks @akreao. (#454, #455, #456)
- **Companion rebirth:** companions that reach base 99 / job 50 in a 2nd job can be reborn, and it's your choice. A new Rebirth tab in the companion panel (or `@companion rebirth <name> novice|trans`) either starts them over as a High Novice, as an official rebirth does, or advances them straight to their transcendent class at their current level. Before, companions stayed in their 2nd job for good. Gear the new class can't wear goes back to you. Thanks @clarois. (#460)

## Modding
- **Mods can direct the population's players:** nine new script commands let a mod's NPC script find the AI players around a spot, take one from the AI for a while and drive it with the usual `unitwalk`, `unittalk`, `unitattack` and `pcfollow` commands, spawn a particular one (a rival, a mentor) and send it away, and answer whispers to it. A held player follows its target through portals as a player would. The [Shell control guide](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/blob/main/docs/mods/shell-control.md) has the reference, and the `shell-gz` example has the AI players say "gz" when you level up. Thanks @BlaXun. (#420)
- **A store of a mod's own (experimental):** a mod can keep key/value data for itself, globally, per account or per character, from its NPC scripts (`modstore_set`, `modstore_get`, …), its Lua hooks (`store.*`) and, read-only, its client UI (`api.store.get`). It's saved with the rest of the world and included in backups, and Settings → Mods → **Reset data…** clears one mod's data. `@modstore` shows it in game. See the [Mod store guide](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/blob/main/docs/MOD_STORE.md); it may change before 1.6. (#457, rathena#27, rathena#28)
- **Mods' SQL is read-only:** `query_sql` and `query_logsql` in a mod's scripts now use a login of their own that can read the game's tables, except accounts' logins, and change nothing. No published mod is affected. (#457, rathena#27)
- **What an update changed, for every version you skipped:** Settings → Mods → Updates now shows the notes of every version between yours and the new one, not just the latest. Mods in the mod list can ship a `CHANGELOG.md` with a section per version; mods from GitHub use their release notes. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#437)
- **Friendlier refining (new in the mod list):** refines that fail no longer break the item unless you turn breaking back on, and you choose the level from which a failed refine drops it one level instead. Thanks @BlaXun. (#426)
- **Affix Forge and Quick Move (new in the mod list):** Affix Forge gives equipment that monsters drop random bonus attributes and adds an Affix Smith in Prontera to move, remove and reroll them; Quick Move moves items between Inventory, Storage, the cart, Equipment, vending, trades and mail with Ctrl+click. Thanks @rhewid. (#427, #429)

## Fixes
- **Mercenaries drawn as only a head:** a hired mercenary is drawn with its body again. The same fix should also bring back the Mechanic's robots, the warg and a New Year doll wherever they were missing. (roBrowserLegacy#88 via #441)
- **Switching era:** after switching between renewal and pre-renewal, item descriptions and signboards showed the old era's (a pre-renewal Jacket read "Defense: 15"). With the game closed, Apply now clears the client's cache. With it open, Settings says so and offers a **Reopen game** button. Thanks @akreao. (#439)
- **Fast double clicks in shops:** double-clicking down a list in an NPC shop or a vending window, to buy or sell one item after another, no longer drops every double click after the first until you pause. Thanks @rhewid, whose quick-move mod found the cause. (roBrowserLegacy#87 via #436)
- **Game Start button:** on character select, the "Game Start" label sits in the middle of its button again instead of on top, and the button no longer takes clicks below its picture. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#89 via #451)
- **Navigation preview:** picking one location of a search result now shows that NPC's own sprite. Two NPCs of the same name, such as the Arena Managers in the arena and in Morocc, used to both show the first one's. Thanks @akreao. (#453)
