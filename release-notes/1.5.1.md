Guild emblems, level auras past 99 (with the gold 4th-job aura), population players that pick up their own loot, skies, weather and music for custom maps, and a dynamic market for Prontera's vendors, plus fixes for companion buffs, pre-renewal signboards, iRO clients' item names, cloth colours, choosing a client folder, the Basic Info menu, a stuck Mods page, the Basic Info window after a job change, and lists that scrolled too far with a touchpad or smooth wheel.

## New
- **Guild emblems:** a guild master can now upload a guild emblem, and other players see it. Thanks @akreao. (#394)
- **The population picks up loot:** the population's ambient players can now walk over and pick up the drops around them, set up in a new Settings → Population → Loot section. It's off by default. Thanks @BlaXun. (#409)
- **Level auras past 99:** high-level characters no longer all wear the same blue level-99 aura. Level 150 and 160 get bigger auras of their own, transcendent jobs at 99 get the higher one, and a 4th job at 250 gets the gold aura. The coloured auras costume items give now show too, and a server can set its own aura colours. (#418, roBrowserLegacy#86)
- **Mod release notes on the Updates tab:** an update for a mod from GitHub now shows its release notes in full on Settings → Mods → Updates, under "What's new", instead of only the first few lines in the confirmation box. Removing a mod, and choosing a folder or an archive to add one from, are now asked in the Settings window itself. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#431)

## Modding
- **Custom maps get a sky, weather and music:** a mod's `mod.json` can now give each of its maps a sky and cloud colour, a weather effect and its own BGM with a new `"maps"` key, instead of a black void and the default track. A new [Custom maps guide](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/blob/main/docs/mods/CUSTOM_MAPS.md) covers making one end to end. (#411, roBrowserLegacy#85)
- **Dynamic market (prontera-vendors 1.4.0):** an optional market where the population's vendor and buying-store prices move with what players actually trade. Prices drift back over time, and occasional market news pushes groups of items up or down. A Market Board in Prontera and `@market` show the movers. It's off by default; turn it on with the mod's `dynamic_market` setting. Thanks @BlaXun. (#387)
- **Per-item price factor:** the population engine reads `$@pop_item_pct[<item id>]` (a percent) for its vendors, buying stores and customers, so any mod or event script can raise or lower an item's price. (#387)

## Fixes
- **Companion buffs:** companions cast Blessing, Increase AGI, Kyrie Eleison and their other ally buffs on themselves again, not only on the people around them. Thanks @akreao. (#399)
- **Companions stuck on a refused skill:** a companion no longer keeps casting a skill the server refuses on that ally, such as Providence on a Crusader, Marionette Control on a Bard or Dancer of the same sex, or a Chemical Protection for gear the ally isn't wearing. Thanks @akreao. (#400)
- **Huge skill damage:** a skill hitting for more than 2,147,483,647 showed no damage number at all. It now shows the cap, the same as a basic attack. Thanks @akreao. (rathena#26 via #407, fixes #389)
- **Pre-renewal signboards:** in pre-renewal, the signboards and icons over NPCs no longer show the renewal ones. This affected Linux. Thanks @akreao. (#378)
- **Pre-renewal server log:** a pre-renewal server no longer logs hundreds of unknown items and skills at start-up for the 3rd and 4th classes it doesn't have. Thanks @akreao. (#402)
- **Item names with an iRO client:** with a recent iRO client and the English translation on, items showed as "Unknown Item" in two ways: items only the translation names (kRO's novice boxes, for example), and items only iRO names (its shards, costumes and some cash shop gear). Both are named again: the translation comes first and iRO's own table fills the gaps. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#382, #391)
- **Cloth colours:** jobs whose game data has fewer cloth palettes than the stylist offers (High Priest, Sniper and Lord Knight on iRO, for example) used to show the default colour for the missing ones. Those colours now get a green, brown, black and purple of their own. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#398)
- **Choosing a client folder:** picking a new client folder on the setup screen now replaces every file the last one set, and clears the ones the new folder doesn't have. Before, switching from kRO to iRO kept kRO's `rdata.grf`, and files from both clients were served together. (#393)
- **Basic Info menu:** with the Companions button added, the menu's buttons spilled out below the window. The menu now scrolls, button names show below it, and non-4th classes get the Reputation Status button too. Thanks @akreao. (#398)
- **Mods page:** after a mod was removed, or updated to a version with its own settings page, the Mods page kept saying options had changed through every Apply. That's fixed. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#380)
- **After a job change:** the Basic Info window now shows the new job level, experience and HP/SP/AP bars straight away, instead of the old ones or nothing. Thanks @akreao. (#397)
- **HP/SP bars:** a quick run of updates could leave a bar showing an older value's colour and width over the newer one. Thanks @akreao. (#397)
- **Clicking the map:** a window that closed while the pointer was over it could stop the map from taking clicks until it was opened again. Thanks @akreao. (#397)
- **Scrolling lists:** shops, vending, storage, the cart, chat and other lists now scroll by how far the wheel moves, not once per wheel event, so a touchpad or a smooth wheel no longer runs a list to its end in one flick. The NPC shop no longer stops between rows either. Thanks @rhewid, whose scroll-fix mod found the cause. (#406)
- **4th-job effects:** Hasty Fire in the Hole, Enchanting Sky and Dark Dragon - Nightmare now draw their effects. Thanks @akreao. (#397)
