Mod status icons, Lua zeny payments and an MVP card drop rate of their own, with monsters on the newer maps dropping loot again and Druid, Karnos and Alitea growing properly.

## New
- **MVP cards get a drop rate of their own:** Settings → Rates has an MVP card drop rate apart from Card drops, so ordinary cards can be generous while MVP cards stay rare. Until you change it, it follows Card drops, as before. (#665)
- **Advanced server settings:** a new Advanced box under Rates takes any of rAthena's battle settings as `key: value` lines. Apply tells you which lines the server couldn't read and which sliders a line overrides. (#665)

## Modding
- **regional-items has left the app's registry:** it moves to its author's own repository. Copies already installed keep working, but no longer get updates from here. (#667)
- **Status icons of a mod's own:** a mod can give its statuses icons the client has never had, numbered 20000–22999, by shipping its own status icon tables; the server sends them and the client shows them with their stack count. A mod file with a Lua error is now left out on its own instead of taking the stock icons with it. See docs/mods/CUSTOM_SKILLS.md. Thanks @BlaXun. (rathena#71, roBrowserLegacy#203, #668 via #670)
- **Lua can pay zeny:** a mod's Lua hooks can give a player zeny with `c:give_zeny(amount, who)`, beside `c:give_item`. It never goes past the most a character can hold. Thanks @BlaXun. (rathena#75 via #676, #674)

## Fixes
- **Druid, Karnos and Alitea:** their HP and SP now grow with level instead of being placeholders, six of their skills cost the SP and AP they do on kRO, and Baby Karnos stops at level 200 like the other baby 4th jobs. The HP and SP are estimated from kRO play where official values aren't known yet. Thanks @akreao. (rathena#70 via #666)
- **Newer monsters drop loot again:** 124 monsters on the newer maps, such as the Yorker Workers on Jormungandr's base and the Episode 21 sea monsters, dropped nothing but their card because no data source lists their drops. They now drop what their closest relative does, and keep their own card. Thanks @LilArt420. (rathena#73 via #673)
- **Mods that declare the same quest no longer break it:** when two mods (or a mod and the stock tables) declared the same quest, every target was added a second time, so the quest log showed each objective twice, and a quest with two or three targets failed to load with "Targets list exceeds the maximum of 3". A quest declared again now just updates its targets. Thanks @LilArt420. (rathena#74 via #675)
