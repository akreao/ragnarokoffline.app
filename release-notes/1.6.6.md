The world gets bigger with 105 more maps and 458 more monsters, and the regional items from 1.6.4 now have proper names, sprites and descriptions.

## New
- **A fuller world:** 105 official maps that the game client already carried but the server never loaded (the Chapter 2 safe zones, the Abyss and Biosphere maps, Jormungandr's temple and more) are now open, with their monsters, spawns, portals, transport and service NPCs. 458 monsters that were missing are filled in, and navigation knows the new monsters, NPCs and warps. Prontera also gets a Rune Tablet NPC (Prontera 140,190) for kRO's Rune Book, rune sets, upgrades and decomposition. Only maps rAthena adds or doesn't use are touched, and pre-renewal is unchanged. Thanks @LilArt420. (rathena#54 via #635)
- **Regional Items in the registry:** a new Renewal mod gives the regional items from 1.6.4 their client text: English names and descriptions for 237 kRO-only items, iRO's wording for 8 items such as Fifth Element and Mask of Ifrit, and names, sprites and stat descriptions for 3,582 items that showed as "Unknown Item". Thanks @LilArt420. (#633)
