Fixes the alternative outfit in the character select preview and the stylist window after a refused look.

## Fixes
- **Alternative outfits on character select:** a character wearing a 3rd-class alternative outfit is now drawn in it in the character select preview, not only once you're in the game. Thanks @MondoTruth. (roBrowserLegacy#148 via #591)
- **Stylist after a refused look:** when the stylist refused a look (usually not enough Zeny or a missing coupon), every later Apply in the same window did nothing while the preview stayed on your character, so the look appeared bought until you changed maps. A refusal now puts your look back and Apply works again. The message also says why a look is usually refused. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#592)
