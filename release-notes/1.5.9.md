Companions can roam and use their class skills, a fallback GRF for sprites your client lacks, gamepad improvements, a repair for damaged data disks and safer Apply, plus fixes for character select, selection boxes, chat line breaks, the minimap and Unequip All.

## New
- **Companions can roam:** a new Settings → Population option lets companions walk the map on their own, the way the fields' fake players do, instead of following you. Turn it on and the Companions window offers **Roam** per companion and for the whole party. Roaming companions fight around themselves, stay in your party, follow you between maps and come back with **Recall**. It's off by default. A companion that dies now also says so in party chat, with where it fell, and the Companions window marks it ✝ until it's revived. Thanks @BlaXun. (#536)
- **Fallback GRF (advanced):** Settings can now point at a second client's `data.grf`. It's read only for files your own client doesn't have, such as sprites of items or monsters that are too new for it. Item names and other tables still come from your client. It's for people who know what they're doing, because mixing two clients can cause problems that are hard to track down. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#499)
- **Companions of more classes use their skills:** Gunslinger, Ninja, Taekwon, Star Gladiator, Soul Linker, Super Novice and the high first classes had no skill list and only auto-attacked, as companions and as field characters. They fight with their own skills now. Field characters of classes without a list of their own also stop borrowing the Swordman's gear and skills. Thanks @BlaXun. (#571)
- **A drafted companion comes at your level:** with Free choice, a new companion starts at your base level instead of a level rolled for its class. Thanks @BlaXun. (#571)

## Gamepad
- **Gamepad improvements:** skills and items can be dropped onto the gamepad HUD, a released right stick keeps its aim target, there are new "Players" and "NPCs & players" target categories, and hidden monsters and players can no longer be targeted. Thanks @BlaXun. (roBrowserLegacy#125 via #564)

## Modding
- **Companion strategy tables:** new opt-in keys for companion party plans: `Role` lists, `NotOwner`, `Skill` on a casting enemy, `Boss`, `Mob`, `Depth`, `UseItem` (use an item from the companion's bag), `MoveTo: { Sight }`, `Aim` for ground skills, `Target: { Field }`, and `Reach`/`Requires` that follow rAthena's own checks. The trace now says why a cast wasn't made. Thanks @BlaXun. (#563)
- **Barter shops from mods:** a mod can add barter shops by shipping `db/import/barters.yml`. Thanks @akreao. (rathena#32 via #565)
- **Lua skill hooks for level and healing:** a mod's Lua skill hooks can now change the skill level, the job level a skill sees and the amount it heals. With no hook, nothing changes. Thanks @BlaXun. (rathena#31 via #567)
- **prontera-vendors 1.6.0:** a new **Stall density** setting spreads stalls over more lanes or packs them together. Pre-renewal stalls and buyers no longer list renewal-only items. **Hunted supply**, **Start with a filled market**, **MvP kills** and **Market news** are settings of their own again. After updating, check **Hunted supply** (it starts off) and **Market news** once; the mod's changelog explains why. Thanks @BlaXun. (#550)

## Fixes
- **Applying mods or settings no longer rolls progress back:** Apply, switching a mod on and a restore used to switch the game servers off hard, losing everything since the last autosave. The servers now save and stop cleanly first. (#569)
- **A damaged data disk is repaired at start:** if the VM died mid-write and left its data disk damaged, every later start failed with "the virtual machine did not come up". The disk is now checked and repaired at boot, and **Report a problem** includes the previous run's VM logs too. (nebula 0.2.7 via #573)
- **Character select stalls:** on a cold start, character select no longer freezes for a second or more while the item database loads. The preview now loads only the headgear and garment tables it needs, and only when a character wears one the built-in tables don't know. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#134 via #562)
- **iRO pictures and footprints:** about 450 pictures stored uncompressed in iRO's `data.grf`, including some NPC illustrations and the plain footprint effect, are served again. Thanks @akreao. (RemoteClient#9, v1.0.5, via #529)
- **iRO's English tables:** a client table naming entries the client's own lists lack (iRO's English pet info and random-option names) now keeps the rest of its entries instead of losing the whole table. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#106 via #564)
- **Selection boxes:** Renewal boxes that let you pick your reward, such as the Metal Weapon Box and the Booster hat and garment boxes, now open their selection window even when your client's tables don't list them. Your client's own definitions are kept. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (#501, roBrowserLegacy#107 via #564)
- **Companions and ground skills:** a companion no longer starts a ground skill on a cell that's already taken, which used to spend the catalyst for nothing, and a fight that has just ended gets one more turn before the companion rests. Thanks @BlaXun. (#563)
- **Skill levels on pre-renewal:** on pre-renewal with the English translation, Fire Bolt and most other skills showed no level arrows in the skill window, so you couldn't pick a lower level to cast. They're back. Clicking an arrow also no longer reserves a skill point. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#135 via #568)
- **Line breaks in chat:** multi-line messages, such as the `/help` command list, show one entry per line again instead of one run-on paragraph. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#136 via #574)
- **Town services on the minimap:** with current kRO or translated town tables, the minimap and navigation window listed only one service per town. They list all of them now. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#137 via #574)
- **Unequip All works:** the Equipment window's **Unequip All** button now takes off everything you're wearing. It shows only on the General and Costume tabs. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#142 via #576)
