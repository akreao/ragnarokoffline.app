Choose your own command prefix, a smaller and faster client with akreao's performance work, Rune Tablet, Black Sun and the renewal endgame mods in the registry, and World Sync monsters that fight and drop like their peers.

## New
- **Choose your command prefix, and storage for everyone:** Settings → Rates has a new *Command prefix*, so in-game commands can start with `!` or another symbol instead of `@`, the way many private servers do it. The Player Commands mod can also let accounts without GM rights open their storage with `@storage`. (#648)
- **Black Sun in the registry:** a Renewal instance on Mt. Mjolnir for a solo player or a party. John Wolf in Prontera starts it, and it runs through three hordes, the Alpha Werewolves and a climb to the Black Sun MVP. It's for base level 200. Thanks @LilArt420. (#650)
- **World Sync Extras in the registry:** optional extras for World Sync, each behind its own setting: entrances into the World Sync maps (off by default, since they need those maps in your client), 740 official NPCs placed with their own sprite, name and spot, and `@npcplace` for GMs to move an NPC and keep it there. Renewal. Thanks @LilArt420. (#651)
- **Regional Items 2.0.1:** 366 item names no longer end in a database note such as "# !todo check english name". Thanks @LilArt420. (#652)
- **Rune Tablet in the registry:** the Rune Tablet in its kRO window, with Tablets, an Encyclopedia and Disassembly. You can register runes, build their sets and upgrade them to +15, and break items down into rune materials. It runs on the server's own Rune Tablet, so your progress carries over. The Rune Tablet now stands at Prontera 193,189, with or without the mod. Renewal. Thanks @LilArt420. (#656, Flux159/rathena#68 via #657)
- **Renewal endgame mods in the registry:** eight pre-renewal mods that take the game from 99 to 175 with renewal's third and expanded classes, lands, monsters, bosses, gear and cards, balanced against pre-renewal. They install from BlaXun's own repositories (ro-endgame-classes and ro-endgame-world). The minibosses and MVPs need 1.6.8. Thanks @BlaXun. (#646)
- **Faster map loading and drawing, from akreao's work upstream:** akreao's performance work was merged into upstream roBrowserLegacy (MrAntares/roBrowserLegacy#1513–#1521) and comes in with the upstream sync: a map asks for its ground, models and textures as soon as their names are known, windows fetch their art when they're first drawn, the frame limit paces to whole display refreshes, characters and monsters are drawn in a few batched calls from shared sprite and palette atlases, and there's an opt-in performance probe. Also from upstream: long hair stays in front of the body when sitting. Thanks @akreao. (Flux159/roBrowserLegacy#202 via #662)
- **A smaller client download:** the client's main script is minified and served compressed, about 1.6 MB instead of 3.2 MB. Thanks @akreao. (#600)
- **Instant map changes, if you want them:** Settings → General → Movement has a new switch, off by default, that drops the fade to and from the loading image on warps, teleports and Fly Wings. The fade is how official clients do it; the switch is for anyone who liked the old snap. (#660)
- **Smaller installers:** about 20 MB less, from dropping a database plugin the server never uses. (#661)

## Modding
- **Two mods can both give monsters skills:** when two enabled mods each ship `db/mob_skill_db.txt`, the app now joins their rows (the later mod's after the earlier's) instead of letting the later file replace the earlier one, so neither mod's monsters lose their skills. Thanks @BlaXun. (#645)
- **More skills follow a mod's level hook:** Prestige, Freezing Spell and Deep Sleep Lullaby now ask a mod's Lua `level` hook (and the Lullaby its `job_level` hook) instead of reading the base level directly, so a level cap set by a mod applies to them too. With no hook, nothing changes. Thanks @BlaXun. (Flux159/rathena#66 via #647)

## Fixes
- **Riding characters shown on foot in the stylist and the skill window:** while you ride a Peco Peco, a dragon or another mount, the stylist's preview and hair thumbnails and the skill window's sitting figure showed the mount. They now show your character on foot. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#643, Flux159/roBrowserLegacy#199 via #644)
- **World Sync monsters show their own sprites:** on an iRO client, 289 World Sync monsters (Episode 20/21, Varmundt, Chapter 2) and the training-ground dummies were all drawn as the same stand-in sprite. The client now knows the sprite names of 717 kRO-only monsters and NPC looks, so they appear as themselves when your kRO data has their art. Thanks @LilArt420. (Flux159/roBrowserLegacy#200 via #654)
- **The chat keeps its history when you change maps:** since 1.6.7, every warp or teleport emptied the chat window's tabs and the Up/Down history of what you'd typed. Both now stay until you go back to character select or quit. Thanks @LilArt420. (Flux159/roBrowserLegacy#201 via #655)
- **World Sync monsters fight like their peers:** some World Sync monsters had been given the HP, attack and EXP of an unrelated monster, so a normal Yorker Worker had a boss's 207 million HP and 29 high-level monsters hit for 0. Missing values are now estimated from monsters of the same kind near in level and HP. Thanks @LilArt420. (Flux159/rathena#69 via #658)
- **World Sync monsters drop what they should:** where Divine Pride doesn't show a World Sync monster's drop chances, they're now estimated instead of left out. Thanks @LilArt420. (Flux159/rathena#67 via #659)
