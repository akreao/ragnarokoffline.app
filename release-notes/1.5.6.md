Costume auras and sprite effects drawn like the official client's, footprint effects, an option to skip the Teleport Lv 1 menu, and renewal's new lands opened to pre-renewal, plus fixes for coloured auras, dark auras, costume previews on character select, hat effects with a recent client, and item names on Linux with an iRO client.

## New
- **More costume auras:** the job-coloured level-99 and level-160 auras that costume items give, and the gray and midnight blue ones, now draw instead of nothing. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#105 via #518)
- **Aura colours like the client's:** the coloured and job-coloured auras now use the exact colours the official client gives them, and the tiger and level-99 midnight blue auras draw too. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#108 via #527)
- **Costume sprite effects:** sixteen costume hat effects drawn as a sprite on the character now show, among them Valkyrie Wings, Black Thunder, the Subject Auras, Serpentine Vision, Aura of Ghost Ship and Beelzebub's Wing. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#109 via #527)
- **Footprints:** footprint costume effects such as Puppy Footprint now leave their prints as you walk, including the plain picture footprints. Settings → Graphics has a "View my footprints only" option to hide everyone else's. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#111, #112 via #527; #113, #114 via #530)
- **Instant Teleport Lv 1:** a new Settings option skips the Random / Cancel menu when you cast Teleport level 1, so it warps at once as a Fly Wing does. That includes Teleport from a Creamy Card. It's off by default. Thanks @akreao. (#534, fixes #533)
- **Renewal's lands in pre-renewal:** a new renewal-world mod in the registry opens Bifrost, Mora, Eclage, El Dicastes, Verus, Dewata, Port Malaya, Malangdo, Rockridge and new dungeon floors to pre-renewal, reached where renewal puts their entrances. An option also opens the areas renewal keeps behind episode quests. This first version only opens the way: the new maps have no monsters, shops or quests yet. Thanks @BlaXun. (#537)

## Modding
- **Mods' command grants:** a mod that gives a group a command rAthena's own groups already grant under another name (`@autolootitem`, `@ksprotection`, `@char_block`) no longer has that group's other grants thrown out by the server. Thanks @akreao. (#517)

## Fixes
- **Costumes on character select:** after a full restart, the character-select preview now shows newer costume headgear and garments too, not only the look the built-in tables know. Thanks @MondoTruth. (roBrowserLegacy#103 via #515)
- **Hat effects with a recent client:** the app served the English translation's older hat effect table, which knew only the first 225 effects, so newer ones and the footprint effects drew nothing. The client's own table is used now. Thanks @akreao. (#516)
- **Taking off an aura costume:** a coloured aura now goes away when the item that gave it comes off. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#104 via #518)
- **Coloured costume auras look like the client's:** the coloured level-99 and level-160 costume auras are now blended as the official client blends them, and the level-99 ones use the client's own coloured ground glow, without the extra orbs. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#115 via #531)
- **Dark auras:** black and other dark auras no longer draw as black squares or with an orange tint. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#110 via #527)
- **Item names with the client's own text on Linux:** with Original client text and an iRO client, items had no names on a case-sensitive disk, because the client asks for `itemInfo.lub` and iRO ships `iteminfo.lub`. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (#526, fixes #525)

## Known issues
- **Some iRO picture footprints and illustrations may still not show:** about 450 pictures stored uncompressed in iRO's `data.grf` (some NPC illustrations and the plain footprint effect among them) are still not served in this release. The fix is merged in the asset server but not released yet, so it ships in the next version.
