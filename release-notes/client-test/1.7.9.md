More skill effects checked against the official 2025 kRO client, this time Warlock, Arch Bishop and more of the 3rd jobs, plus official effect sounds and fixes for lag during big fights. Please play as you normally would and report anything that looks or acts wrong.

**Before you install:** this build installs as its own app, **Ragnarok Offline Client Test**, beside Ragnarok Offline. It has its own characters, settings and mods, so your main app's saves are never touched. If you already have the Client Test app, it updates itself.
- **To bring your characters over:** in Ragnarok Offline, go to Settings → Save Data → **Back up everything…**. Then in the Client Test app, choose **Restore everything…** and pick that file.
- **Run one app at a time.** Both use the same ports, so quit one before starting the other.

## What to test

### Skill effects
- **Warlock:** Jack Frost, Comet, Crimson Rock, Tetra Vortex (with its four elemental balls), Hell Inferno, Frost Misty, Stasis, Marsh of Abyss, White Imprison, Release, Recognized Spell, Reading Spellbook and Telekinesis Intense now look and sound like the official client.
- **Arch Bishop:** Renovatio's columns of light, Duple Light's orbs, Epiclesis's rings round the tree and Convenio's cast aura.
- **Rune Knight and Ranger:** Sonic Wave rings, Hundred Spear, the green body flash, cast auras and Unlimit.
- **Sounds:** 3rd and 4th job effects now play their own official sounds on the right frames.
- **Timing:** effects that land with the damage (Crimson Rock's meteor, for example) now land exactly when the damage shows.
- **Older skills:** Heal, Blessing, Increase/Decrease AGI, Sight, Aura Blade, Two-Hand Quicken, Asura Strike's images, Eternal Chaos and Loki's Veil, kick and spear hit lines, True Sight, Gravitation Field's spikes, Star Gladiator's Union, Feeling and Hatred, and the Summoner's Tuna Party.
- **/mineffect:** Warlock and Rune Knight skills, Mammonite and Detoxify now use the official reduced effects.
- **Mercenaries** use their own skill effects instead of the player's.

### Windows
- **Item links:** clicking a link again shows the description again instead of closing it.
- **Memo map:** click facilities and quest NPCs to walk there, a boss icon, and a message on maps that don't allow it.
- **Cash shop:** the official cart limits and purchase messages.
- **Guild:** a member's menu offers party invite, 1:1 chat and mail.

### Performance
Busy fights with many skill effects and damage numbers should stutter less: effect textures and damage numbers are shared instead of made again each time, and the minimap and swap window redraw less. Players and monsters walking round a wall no longer zip along it several times too fast. Tell us if anything still lags, and on which map and job.

## Known issues
- Installers aren't signed yet. Windows shows a SmartScreen warning (More info → Run anyway), and on macOS you need System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway the first time.
- 4th jobs other than Star Emperor and Soul Reaper haven't been checked skill by skill.
