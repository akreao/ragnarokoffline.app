The first client-test build: Ragnarok Offline 1.7.7 with Steven's roBrowser fork, where skill effects, windows and many small behaviours were checked one by one against the official 2025 kRO client. Please play as you normally would and report anything that looks or acts wrong.

**Before you install:** this build installs as its own app, **Ragnarok Offline Client Test**, beside Ragnarok Offline. It has its own characters, settings and mods, so your main app's saves are never touched, and you can go back to the main app at any time. It updates only from these client-test releases.
- **To bring your characters over:** in Ragnarok Offline, go to Settings → Save Data → **Back up everything…**. Then in the Client Test app, choose **Restore everything…** and pick that file.
- **Run one app at a time.** Both use the same ports, so quit one before starting the other.
- The Client Test app asks for your game client folder the first time, like a new install.

## What to test

### Skill effects
Most skills of the 1st, 2nd and transcendent jobs, plus Taekwon, Star Gladiator, Soul Linker, Gunslinger/Rebellion, Ninja/Kagerou/Oboro, Summoner, Star Emperor and Soul Reaper, now play what the official client plays, at the same moment:
- **Only when the skill works:** buffs and support skills show nothing on a failed or missed use.
- **Hits:** a hit flash on every hit of a multi-hit skill, the skill's own hit effect on the first, and no hit effects on targets that only took splash damage.
- **Timing:** effects start when the damage arrives, not when the cast starts, with the official sounds on the official frames.
- **Official art:** for example Cold Bolt and Fire Bolt's falling bolts, Arrow Shower's arrows, Shield Boomerang's thrown shield, Rapid Smiting's shields, Soul Strike's souls, alchemist bottles, Bard and Dancer song auras, Sage ground fields, trap explosions, falcon flights and Crimson Marker's marks.
- **Cast circles and auras:** the ground circle is sized per skill, and many skills have their official cast aura.
- **Lasting looks:** body glows and sparks while a status is on (Soul Linker spirits, Warmth, Kaahi/Kaizel, Sight Blaster, Star Emperor stances, Soul Reaper souls and more).
- **4th jobs:** Star Emperor and Soul Reaper were checked skill by skill. Other 4th jobs and Druid got shared fixes only, so tell us what looks off.

### Windows
- **Login, server and character screens:** the 2025 login layout, a 24-character limit on ID and password, a prompt before quitting, the last-played character selected, and deletion countdowns that keep running.
- **Inventory:** the X closes it like its shortcut, cash shop items ask before opening, and Ctrl + right-click identifies an item with a Magnifier.
- **Basic Info:** weight turns red at the server's overweight limit, low HP/SP shows in red when the window is reduced, and Exp reads 100.0% at max level.
- **Hotbar:** the 2025 look, a second bar (Switch Hotkey Bar), Ctrl+F12 to shift rows, and drag a skill off to remove it.
- **Stats, equipment and swap windows:** reduce by double-clicking the title, Shift+click a worn item to link it in chat, and Ctrl+double-click in the swap window to wear one item.
- **Skill tree:** the official layout with your sitting character, and a prompt before spending planned points.
- **Minimap:** the view button and Maximize open a memo map, and World Map opens the world map.
- **Chat:** Page Up/Down scroll the log, and Ctrl jumps to the top or bottom.
- **Others:** the whisper window resizes, the cart and trade windows behave like the official ones, and NPC dialogs ignore double-clicks on Next. Pets, homunculi, mercenaries, mail, store search, attendance and item info windows also got smaller fixes.
- **Tip box:** the tip of the day window, with English tips.

### New features
- **Private Airship:** pay with a World Tour Ticket from the Navigation window to fly to a map.
- **/navi across maps:** routes now work between maps, including through script portals.
- **Champion monsters** show their size and prefix looks, and monsters no longer get a player's level aura.
- **Garments** draw over or under the body according to the official tables.
- **Druid beast forms** hide the garment, weapon and shield.
- **Transfer restrictions:** in an item's info window, a click opens the box that shows where the item can't be moved (trade, storage, cart and so on).
- **Discord chat bridge:** an in-game #discord channel joined to a Discord channel.
- **Non-English character names:** in Rates → Advanced, set `char_name_option` to 0 to allow Japanese and other non-ASCII names.

### Performance
Sprites and auras draw in fewer batches, maps load behind the loading screen, walking starts as soon as you click, and the frame limit follows your display's refresh rate. Tell us if anything stutters or flickers.

## Known issues
- Installers aren't signed yet. Windows shows a SmartScreen warning (More info → Run anyway), and on macOS you need System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway the first time.
- 4th jobs other than Star Emperor and Soul Reaper haven't been checked skill by skill.
