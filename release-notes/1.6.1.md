Adds two new mods to the registry and fixes alternative outfits, ranged-damage random options and a batch of achievements on Renewal.

## Mods
- **Two new mods in the registry:** **Companion Party Overhaul** makes your companions a real party, with class roles, orders in party chat, item use, talk and a Companions window (Alt+N). **Adventurer's Chronicle** is an 8-chapter pre-renewal story questline from level 1 to Odin's Temple, with a journal (Alt+B). Both install and update from their author's own releases. Thanks @AlwaysGH. (#586)

## Fixes
- **Alternative outfits:** on Renewal, the third classes' alternative outfits (`@bodystyle`, or a script's `setlook LOOK_BODY2`) apply and stay after you log in again. Before, the server said "Appearance changed" and nothing happened. On the client:
  - you now see your own outfit after logging in;
  - Arch Bishops and Cardinals get the Arch Bishop outfit instead of the Novice's;
  - a job change no longer leaves the old outfit on until you relog.

  Thanks @MondoTruth. (rathena#38 via #585, roBrowserLegacy#147 via #584)
- **Ranged damage random options:** on Renewal, the random options that raise ranged damage dealt (204) and lower ranged damage taken (205) now work. Before, equipment that rolled them got no bonus. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (rathena#39 via #587)
- **Achievements:** on Renewal, "First step of job change!" is now earned by changing to a transcendent first job, as the client describes it, instead of repeating the ordinary first-job achievement. The Izlude Dungeon and Prontera Culvert treasure achievements also count on the right maps now. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (rathena#43 via #588)
- **More achievements work:** on Renewal, the Morocc, Ferlock and Faceworm Queen hunting achievements count the right monster the right number of times, the Prontera Castle banquet achievements complete once you've met everyone, "Married with who..?" is earned on marriage, and "Magic Insanity" grants the Magic Candy effect its icon shows. On pre-renewal, "First step of job change!" now needs a transcendent first job, as on Renewal. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (rathena#44 via #589)
- **Vending achievements add up:** the "Activating the market economy" achievements now count everything you spend at vendors, instead of only a single purchase of the full amount. On Renewal their targets follow kRO's (10k, 1m, 5m, 100m and 500m Zeny). Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (rathena#45 via #589)
