Fixes 1.5.9's "Some of your game data could not be read" at start-up, and adds companions that start unequipped with spare gear, a bigger inventory, windows that look closer to the official client, and fixes for random options and the Grace armour enchant.

## New
- **Companions can start unequipped:** a new Settings → Population option, **Companions start unequipped**, makes a companion you draft or hire wear only what you trade it, instead of coming dressed for its class. With **Companion inventory** on it can also carry spare pieces: the Companions window's new **Gear** tab lets you carry a worn piece as a spare, wear a carried one, or take things back. It's off by default, and companions you already have keep the way they started. Thanks @BlaXun. (#572)
- **A bigger inventory:** the inventory window can now grow by two more rows, as it already could by two more columns. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#144 via #580)
- **Windows look closer to the official client:** window captions use the regular weight, and the client font's regular and bold files are no longer swapped. Tooltips share one style. The Basic Information bar's text is centred and its menu hints sit above the icons. The equipment tabs, labels and hints, the inventory's counters, controls and tab background, and the Status and Trait values all line up, with better stat hints. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#138, #139, #140, #141, #143, #145 via #580)

## Modding
- **Companion strategies can switch gear:** a new `Equip` rule puts on a carried piece (weapon, shield, armour, garment, shoes, headgear, accessory) while the rule applies, and puts back what it replaced afterwards, e.g. a Manteau while a boss is near. It works for companions that started unequipped, with Companion inventory on. Items can be named by AegisName or by id anywhere a table names one. Thanks @BlaXun. (#572)
- **Attendance rewards from scripts:** a new server extension, `attendance_script_rewards` (off by default), lets a mod's scripts read and change the attendance rewards. Thanks @MondoTruth. (rathena#33 via #581)

## Fixes
- **Getting into the game on 1.5.9:** a player whose character list included someone wearing a headgear or garment newer than the built-in tables couldn't enter the game ("Some of your game data could not be read"). The client tables load again. (roBrowserLegacy#146 via #579)
- **Resist all elements:** the random option that resists every element but Neutral now covers Undead too, as on kRO and iRO. Renewal only. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (rathena#34 via #581)
- **Grace armour enchant:** the Grace armour enchant meant to add damage against Medium monsters gave damage reduction from them instead. New enchants roll the damage bonus; ones already on your gear stay as they are. Renewal only. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (rathena#35 via #582)
- **Size resistance options:** the random options that reduce damage from Small, Medium and Large monsters now reduce physical damage only, as their descriptions say. Magic has options of its own. Renewal only. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (rathena#37 via #582)
