A big client update brings the Druid line to life, full gamepad menus, the roulette and floating chat tabs, mods get skills of their own, and Dragon Knights have their job change quest.

## New
- **The Druid line, in the client:** Druid, Karnos and Alitea now play fully. They shapeshift into Werewolf and Wereraptor, their skills have kRO's effects and a skill window, and Alitea has its AP bar. The server side arrived in 1.6.4. Thanks @LilArt420. (roBrowserLegacy#171 via #605)
- **Dragon Knight job change quest:** Rune Knights at Base 200 / Job 70 can take the official quest to become a Dragon Knight, starting with Oscar in `gef_fild08`. It's the first of the 4th job quests. Thanks @MondoTruth. (rathena#63 via #638)
- **Gamepad menus:** a controller can now move through every window, use and carry items, switch tabs, close info windows with B and change sliders with the D-pad. An on-screen keyboard, with a wheel mode and saved phrases, lets you chat without a keyboard. Thanks @BlaXun. (roBrowserLegacy#177–#180 via #605)
- **Start walking before the server answers:** an option under Settings → General → Movement, off by default. Your character sets off the moment you click, and other characters and monsters glide to where the server puts them instead of jumping. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#163 via #605, #639)
- **Roulette:** the roulette window now loads its rewards and works start to finish: spin, claim, and pick up where you left off. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#193, #195 via #605)
- **Floating chat tabs**, and the message log settings match them. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#150 via #605)
- **Catalog store search:** search the catalog and act on the results. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#194 via #605)
- **Cloth colours follow hair colours** where the client has no cloth palette of its own, instead of drawing black blots. Thanks @MondoTruth. (roBrowserLegacy#198 via #605)
- **Monster and NPC titles** are drawn under their names. Thanks @MondoTruth. (roBrowserLegacy#197 via #605)
- **NPC music:** NPCs that play a song now play it. Thanks @BlaXun. (roBrowserLegacy#173, #611)
- **Smoother map changes:** the next map loads while the screen fades to the loading image, and post-processing stays on across map changes. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#156, #160 via #605)
- **Halloween Town in the registry:** a Halloween event map with its own NPCs, prizes and bats, installed from LilArt's releases. Thanks @LilArt420. (#636)
- **The 2026-10-15 upstream roBrowserLegacy update**, with its fixes to water and sprite depth, capes and robes, the Warper's circle and more. (roBrowserLegacy#170 via #605)

## Modding
- **Skills, passives and statuses of a mod's own:** a mod can add new skills a job learns, passives with stat and combat hooks, statuses with stacks and icons, combo windows, and rework or take away stock skills without costing players their points, all in Lua. The client shows them through the mod's own skill tables. See the new Custom skills guide and the `custom-skills` and `third-class-sampler` examples. Thanks @BlaXun. (rathena#52, roBrowserLegacy#176, #618)
- **Achievement tables:** a mod adds achievements beside the client's instead of replacing its whole table. See the new Custom achievements guide. Thanks @BlaXun. (roBrowserLegacy#174, #614)
- **NPC music from scripts:** `playBGM` from an NPC script now reaches the client. Thanks @BlaXun. (roBrowserLegacy#173, #611)

## Fixes
- **Windows updates stay where you installed the app:** an app installed on another drive could update into the default folder and leave the copy you use on the old version. Updates now install over the folder the app runs from. This applies to updates made from 1.6.7 onwards, so the update to 1.6.7 itself can still land in the default folder once. Thanks @Kdye116 for reporting it. (#640, fixes #632)
- **Forced disconnects return to the login screen** cleanly instead of leaving the game stuck. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#151 via #605)
- **Trading:** zeny can go alongside ten items, and item pictures stay on their own rows. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#189, #191 via #605)
- **Vending and buying stores:** vending windows drag together, Escape cancels a store you were setting up, and buying-store rows update after a trade. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#185–#187 via #605)
- **Newer clients:** September 2026 packet framing, wider item ids in companion feeding, guild emblems that stay current, and effects that honour their start delay and sound loops. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#181–#184, #188, #190, #192 via #605)
- **The Purple Star footprint** no longer makes everyone vanish after a kill. Thanks @LilArt420. (roBrowserLegacy#175 via #605)
- **Smaller fixes:** the minimap skips town services with no icon, signboards are dropped on a warp within the same map, and crowds make less garbage each frame. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#152–#154, #167, #168, #196 via #605)

## Known issues
- **With move prediction on, a refused step shows:** when the server turns a step down, for instance right after you are hit, your character visibly walks back to where the server has it. It's off by default.
