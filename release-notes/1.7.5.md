A DPS meter mod, a damage event for mods, and regional gear that equips on the classes its text names.

## Modding
- **A DPS meter, and a `combat:damage` event for mods:** mods can now listen for every hit the client is told about, with who dealt it (you, your homunculus or mercenary, a party member or AI companion, a companion's pet, or someone else). The new **dps-meter** mod in Settings → Mods → Find Mods uses it: DPS for the current fight, total, biggest hit and a row per companion, opened with Alt+N or its DPS button. Thanks @popkdodge. (#718)

## Fixes
- **Regional gear equips on the classes its text names:** 17 iRO items refused classes their own descriptions list, such as the Agni Sword, Thorn Breaker, Fenrir Staff, the Heretic Acolyte and Mage Boots, Divine Mace, Chaos Sword and Purple Haze. "Swordman, Merchant, and Thief Classes" now means all three whole job lines, and level 100 gear that says "Class : Acolyte" now goes to the whole Acolyte line instead of first-job Acolytes only. Thanks @LilArt420. (rathena#84 via #717)
