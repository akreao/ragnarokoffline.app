Fixes for Summoner skill names, the hotbar's skill levels, the Emotion List, NPC dialogs that slowed down over a long session and Lope and Shadow Leap, plus a way for mods to remove a signboard.

## Modding
- **Removing a signboard:** a mod's `customSignBoardList` row with no icon now removes the sign earlier tables put on that cell, so a mod can take a stale sign away. Before, such a row stopped the signboards from loading. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#133 via #557)

## Fixes
- **Summoner skills' names:** Lunatic Carrot Beat cast with a carrot, and the second hit of CN Meteor, showed "Unknown Skill !!" over the caster. They show their names now. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#129 via #553)
- **Hotbar skill levels:** a skill on the hotbar now follows the skill's level when you raise it, as the official client does. Bash put on the bar at level 5 no longer stays at 5 after you take it to 10. Skills whose level you pick, such as Heal or Fire Bolt, keep the level you chose. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#130 via #553)
- **Emotion List:** clicking an emote in the Emotion List did nothing. A click puts its command in the chat box (or the hotbar macro field you're editing) again, and a double click plays it. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#131 via #555)
- **NPC dialogs slowing down:** NPC dialogs opened and advanced more slowly the longer the game ran, until every page had a visible delay after a few hours. Each line of NPC text left a watcher behind on the window; now there's only ever one. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#132 via #556)
- **Lope and Shadow Leap:** when Lope or Shadow Leap couldn't move you (a blocked cell, a GvG or battleground map, Root Twist), your character could look like it had moved anyway. The server now puts it back where it really is. Thanks @akreao. (rathena#30 via #554)

## Known issues
- **Some iRO picture footprints and illustrations may still not show:** about 450 pictures stored uncompressed in iRO's `data.grf` (some NPC illustrations and the plain footprint effect among them) are still not served in this release. The fix is merged in the asset server but not released yet, so it ships in a later version.
